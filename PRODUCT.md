# Product

**Kharadhu** is a Dhivehi-first budgeting app for people in the Maldives. Money is in MVR; amounts are always shown in Latin digits ("MVR 1,234.50") as left-to-right runs. Dhivehi (right-to-left) is the default with a runtime English switch.

## Who it is for
Everyday earners and families who want to see where money goes, stay inside a monthly budget, clear debt, and give sadaqah and zakat. Many read Dhivehi first; some use English.

## What it does
- Log expenses and income, scan receipts with Raseedhu (AI), monthly budgets, reports and multi-month trends.
- Debt-free plan, sadaqah and zakat, and the Wasiyyah and Amanah register for someone who trusts you.

## Brand and tone
Calm, trustworthy, unhurried. Indigo, pearl and midnight; frosted glass. Never alarmist: a budget warning is said in words, not by colour alone. Dhivehi copy is a draft until native-reviewed.

## Platform
Native iOS and Android (Expo / React Native). A small web build exists only for invite, account deletion and password-reset links. Surfaces are task-first (Operate).

## Principles
1. Numbers first: the figure the person came for is the largest thing on screen.
2. Words plus colour: every state is also stated in text.
3. Full RTL and LTR parity; forms for email, password and amounts are always LTR.
4. 44 pt touch targets, Dynamic Type, light and dark, Reduce Motion respected.
