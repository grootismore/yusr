# Customer

Who Kharadhu is for and the job they hire it to do. Extended by the improve-app journey, Phase 1.

## Who
A Dhivehi-speaking person or household in Malé or the atolls who tracks money in MVR; secondary, English-speaking residents. See `docs/kharadhu-prd.md`.

## Job Statement
**Core job:** When I get paid or spend money in MVR, I want to record it without effort in my own language and see where the month is going, so I can stop being surprised when the money runs out.

**Adjacent jobs** (served by optional features that are switched on only when needed):
- When I share costs with a partner, family or friends, I want one clear picture of who paid and who owes, so we stop arguing and settle up fairly. (Household, Hissa Foshi, IOUs, money pools)
- When I owe money or am owed money, I want a plan and a record, so I can become debt-free and stay on good terms. (Debt-free plan, IOUs)
- When I want to give or leave things in order, I want it kept privately and correctly, so my faith and family duties are met. (Giving, zakat, register)

Decided with the owner on 2026-11-21: one core job, the rest adjacent. Hire type to work on: both first use (Big Hire) and daily use (Little Hire).

## Job Dimensions
| Dimension | What the user wants | Where the app underdelivers (audit, not yet user evidence) |
|---|---|---|
| Functional | Log a spend or a payday in seconds, see where the month goes, know what is left | The core paths (keypad, category, Save; scan) are fast, but the screens around them are busy: Home carried up to ten cards until the recent simplification, and reports and budgets sit behind several taps. |
| Emotional (worst gap) | Calm and in control after opening the app, not judged | An over-budget state shows as a red alarm pill on the first screen. The number of features and switches feels like a lot to manage. Success moments are thin: little reward for a good month. |
| Social | Be seen by family as managing money responsibly; keep trust when money is shared | Sharing is spread across four features that look alike (household, Hissa Foshi, IOUs, pools). Settling up depends on friends having the app or on a shared image. |

## Competing Alternatives
| Alternative | Why hired | Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| Not tracking (a rough idea in the head) | No effort | Surprise at month end; no record |
| Paper notebook or Notes app | Free, instant, any language | No totals, no categories, easy to forget, nothing to share |
| Spreadsheet (Excel, Sheets) | Flexible, familiar | Slow on a phone, no scan, no Thaana-friendly flow |
| Bank app or SMS history | Already there | Only card and transfer spending, not cash; no categories in Dhivehi |
| WhatsApp group or chat messages | How friends already settle up | Not a ledger; amounts get lost; no balances |
| English-first expense apps (Receiptix, Splitwise and similar) | Polished, scan, splitting | Not Dhivehi, MVR and local receipts and bills not understood, no local religious duties |
